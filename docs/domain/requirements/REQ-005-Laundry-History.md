# REQ-005 — Laundry History

## Purpose

Define a functional requirement for TenderApp: Laundry History.

## TenderApp Adaptation

The system shall store completed and planned laundry loads so users can review past decisions and results.

## Engineering Notes

Rationale: History enables trust, validation, and future model improvement.
