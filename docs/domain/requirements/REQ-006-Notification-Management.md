# REQ-006 — Notification Management

## Purpose

Define a functional requirement for TenderApp: Notification Management.

## TenderApp Adaptation

The system shall notify users about key events such as ideal hanging time, drying completion, or rain risk.

## Engineering Notes

Rationale: Notifications convert predictions into timely action.
